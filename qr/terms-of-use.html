Product details come from public product databases, from the brand's or retailers' websites, from photos you send, and from AI (Google Gemini) that reads and summarizes them. Scores, ratings and labels are produced automatically.

⚠️ Product results are for general information only. They can be incomplete, out of date or wrong: product recipes change, databases contain errors, and AI can misread a label or make mistakes. Always read the product's own pack before you buy or use it.
  • Not medical or dietary advice: scores, allergen alerts, pregnancy and skin suitability notes and Ask AI answers are not medical, nutritional or dermatological advice. If you have an allergy, a health condition, or are pregnant, check the pack and ask a qualified professional
  • Allergens: an allergen not shown in the app may still be in the product, including traces. Never rely on the app alone if you have an allergy
  • Halal: the halal indication is an estimate from ingredients and labels. It is not a halal certification. "Certified" is shown only when a halal mark was read on a pack photo, and even then the app cannot verify the certifier. For religious decisions, check the certification on the pack or with a halal authority

If you find wrong information, use "Report wrong info" in the app or email us, and we will look into it.
