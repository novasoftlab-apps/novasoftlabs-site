Overview

Product Scanner is free to download and use. Pro is an optional subscription that removes ads and the daily limit on product checks, and adds Ask AI and custom QR styles. Both plans come with a 7-day free trial for eligible users. Pro is sold and billed through Google Play.

💳 Nova Soft Labs never sees or stores your payment details. All billing is handled by Google Play.

03 —

Plans

  • Monthly: billed every month, renews automatically
  • Yearly: billed once a year, renews automatically, and costs less per month than Monthly
  • Limited-time offers: from time to time we may offer a discounted first year on the Yearly plan. The offer price, how long it lasts, and the regular price it renews at are shown before you confirm

The upgrade screen and the Google Play purchase screen show the exact price in your local currency, the billing period and any free trial before you buy. Prices may vary by country and may include taxes.

ℹ️ Pro applies only to this app. It doesn't unlock features in other Nova Soft Labs apps. If you bought Lifetime access when it was offered, it stays valid; it is no longer sold.
04 —

Free Trial

Eligible users get a 7-day free trial on the Monthly or Yearly plan. The app and Google Play show the trial, and the price that follows it, before you confirm.

  • The trial starts when you confirm the subscription on Google Play
  • You are not charged during the trial and get all Pro features
  • When the trial ends, the subscription becomes paid at the price shown when you subscribed
  • To avoid being charged, cancel in Google Play before the trial ends
  • Google Play decides who is eligible, usually one free trial per Google account
⚠️ If you don't cancel before the trial ends, you will be charged for the first billing period. Cancelling later stops future renewals but doesn't refund the current period.
05 —

Billing & Renewal

By subscribing, you authorize Google Play to charge your payment method each billing period until you cancel.

  • Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel before the renewal date
  • If the price changes, Google Play tells you in advance and, where required, asks you to accept the new price
  • If a payment fails, Google Play may retry it and keep Pro active for a short grace period. If payment can't be collected, the subscription is paused or cancelled
📅 See your next renewal date in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
06 —

How to Cancel

You can cancel at any time. You keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

  • Step 1: Open the Google Play Store app
  • Step 2: Tap your profile icon, top right
  • Step 3: Tap Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
  • Step 4: Select Product Scanner
  • Step 5: Tap Cancel subscription and follow the steps

You can also manage subscriptions at play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

⚠️ Uninstalling the app does not cancel your subscription. Cancel in Google Play as described above.
07 —

Refunds

Google Play processes all payments, so refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

  • Within 48 hours of a purchase, you can usually request a refund from Google Play on its refund page
  • After 48 hours, email support@novasoftlabs.com with your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") and we will review your request
  • Eligibility is decided under Google Play's refund policy and applicable consumer law
⚠️ Cancelling stops future renewals; it doesn't refund the current period. If you get a refund, Pro ends.
08 —

Restoring Pro & New Devices

Pro is linked to your Google Play account, not to the app. The app has no separate account.

  • On a new phone or after reinstalling, sign in to Google Play with the same account. Pro comes back automatically, or tap Restore on the Pro screen
  • To keep Pro features safe from tampering, our server confirms your plan with Google Play when you use a Pro feature such as Ask AI. This sends your purchase token, not your name, email or payment details
  • If Pro doesn't show, open the app with an internet connection and tap Restore. If it still doesn't, contact us with your order number
09 —

Managing & Switching Plans

  • View your plan: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions
  • Change payment method: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Payment methods
  • Switch between Monthly and Yearly: cancel your current plan in Google Play, then choose the other plan in the app after your current period ends

Changes to Pro

We may add, improve or change Pro features. Product checks depend on outside providers (such as product databases and Google's AI). If we permanently remove a core Pro feature, we will try to offer a reasonable alternative, and you can cancel at any time. Your rights under Google Play's refund policy and consumer law are not affected.
