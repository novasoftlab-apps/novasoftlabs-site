02 —What the app does

The App turns your Android phone into a remote control and media sender for compatible TVs and streaming devices. Depending on your TV, it lets you:

  • Control your TV with a d-pad, touchpad, air mouse, number pad, volume and channel keys, a keyboard for text entry, and shortcuts that open streaming apps;
  • Use voice search on TVs that support it, by streaming your voice to the TV’s own assistant;
  • Cast photos, videos and music from your phone, and videos found in the built-in Web Video browser, to your TV;
  • Mirror your screen, with sound, to your TV.

We may add, change or remove features at any time, for example to support new TV models or to keep up with changes made by TV manufacturers.

Please note:

  • The App connects over your local Wi-Fi network. It does not use infrared and cannot control TVs that are not connected to the network.
  • Your phone and TV must be on the same network. Guest networks, VPNs, “AP/client isolation” and some corporate or public networks can block the connection.
  • Some TVs ask you to approve the connection or enter a PIN shown on screen. Some must have network control enabled in their own settings.
  • Features differ by brand, model, region and firmware. TV manufacturers can change their software at any time, which may temporarily or permanently stop a feature from working. We do not guarantee that every feature works with every device.
  • Content protected by DRM, and some web videos that depend on cookies or sign-in, cannot be cast.
  • The App is a convenience tool and is not a replacement for your TV’s original remote. Keep your original remote for setup and recovery.

We recommend trying the free version with your TV before buying PRO.

07 —Screen mirroring

When you mirror, everything on your phone’s screen, including notifications, messages and passwords you type, can be seen by anyone watching the TV. Android shows a capture indicator and the App shows a notification for as long as mirroring is running. Stop mirroring before opening private information. Mirroring quality and delay depend on your Wi-Fi network, phone and TV.
