In short

  • Universal Remote has no sign-up and no user account. We do not run servers that receive your remote commands, your media or your screen.
  • Controlling, casting and mirroring happen directly between your phone and your TV over your local Wi-Fi network.
  • Your photos, videos, music, screen and voice are sent only to the TV you choose. They are never uploaded to us.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising identifiers. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first. PRO removes ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

01 —Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Nova Soft (“Nova Soft”, “we”, “us”), the developer of the Universal Remote Android app (the “App”), handles information when you use the App. Nova Soft is the controller of the limited personal data described here. The policy applies to the App as distributed on Google Play and to any related support we provide by email.

The App is a universal remote, casting and screen-mirroring tool for smart TVs and streaming devices, including Google TV, Android TV and Chromecast with Google TV, Samsung smart TVs, LG webOS TVs and Roku TVs and players, as well as Google Cast receivers and DLNA/UPnP media renderers for casting.

By using the App you acknowledge this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App.

04 —Ads and Google AdMob

The free version of the App is supported by full-screen (interstitial) ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. Ads appear at natural pauses after a number of taps, never on the upgrade screens, and never for PRO users.

What AdMob collects

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device’s advertising ID (and, where available, Android Privacy Sandbox signals);
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location may be inferred;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language, screen size, network type and App version;
  • ad interactions such as views, clicks and diagnostic data used to measure ad performance and prevent fraud.

Google uses this information under its own policies. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent

We use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), a Google-certified consent management solution. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, or another region where consent is required, the App shows a consent form before any ad is requested. Depending on your choice, ads may be personalized, non-personalized (based on context only) or limited. Where US state privacy laws give you the right to opt out of targeted advertising, the App honours that choice through the same messaging.

Your ad choices

  • Change your consent at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the App (shown where consent applies).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads). Deleting it stops personalized ads across apps.
  • Remove ads entirely by upgrading to PRO.

The App is not designed for children and does not request child-directed ad treatment. We do not allow ads to be targeted using data about health, religion, or other sensitive categories.

05 —Purchases and subscriptions

PRO subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google collects your payment details, billing address and transaction data under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

We receive from Google Play only what is needed to unlock PRO: the product purchased, whether it is active, a purchase token and an order ID. We never receive your card number or bank details. The App caches your PRO status on your phone so that PRO keeps working without a connection. See our Subscription Policy for billing, trials, cancellation and refunds.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for money. Data leaves your phone only:

  • to the TV or device on your network that you choose to control, cast to or mirror to;
  • to the third-party services listed above, for the purposes listed;
  • where required by law, a court order or a lawful request by public authorities, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or Nova Soft;
  • to a successor entity if Nova Soft or the App is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to your data.

Under some US state laws, allowing ad partners to use advertising identifiers for personalized ads may be considered “selling” or “sharing” personal information. You can opt out at any time as described in Your ad choices.

08 —Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data (remembered TVs, pairing credentials, recent casts, settings, PRO status) is kept until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the App’s storage in Settings → Apps → Universal Remote → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the App.
  • Media, screen content and voice are streamed in real time and are not retained by the App.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own retention policies. You can delete your advertising ID as described above.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required for accounting and legal purposes.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you and for our records, and are deleted on request.

Because the App has no user accounts and we do not hold your data on our servers, there is no account to delete. To ask us to delete any information we hold about you (for example, support emails), write to support@novasoftlabs.com with the subject “Data deletion”. We will respond within 30 days.

09 —Security

Pairing credentials and settings are stored in the App’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Connections to TVs use encryption (TLS) wherever the TV supports it, as Google TV / Android TV, Samsung and LG TVs do. Some devices, such as Roku players and DLNA renderers, only accept unencrypted commands on the local network by design; the App limits this traffic to your local network. Connections to Google and logo.dev use HTTPS.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We recommend using the App only on a Wi-Fi network you trust, keeping your phone and TV software up to date, and remembering that while you mirror your screen, anyone who can see the TV can see your screen, including notifications.

10 —Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, such as 16 in parts of the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Most data stays on your phone, where you control it directly. For anything else, email us at support@novasoftlabs.com. For data held by Google, use your Google Account privacy controls.
