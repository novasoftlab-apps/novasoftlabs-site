In short

  • PRO is optional. It is sold as a Monthly or Yearly subscription, or as a one-time Lifetime purchase.
  • All payments go through Google Play, in your local currency. The exact price, billing period and any trial are shown before you confirm.

01 —Overview

This Subscription Policy explains how paid upgrades work in the Universal Remote Android app (the “App”) by Nova Soft. It forms part of our Terms of Use. All purchases are made with Google Play Billing and are also subject to the Google Play Terms of Service. Where this policy and Google Play’s terms differ on billing, cancellation or refunds, Google Play’s terms apply.

04 —Free trials

  • Some subscription plans may come with a free trial. The trial length is shown on the upgrade screen and in the Google Play purchase sheet before you confirm.
  • Starting a trial requires a valid Google Play payment method, but you are not charged during the trial.
  • When the trial ends, your subscription automatically converts to a paid subscription and you are charged the plan price for each billing period, unless you cancel before the trial ends.
  • If you cancel during the trial, you keep PRO until the trial ends and you are not charged.
  • Trials are generally available once per Google account. Google Play decides eligibility, and we may change or withdraw trials for new purchases at any time.

05 —Special offers

From time to time the App may show a special offer, such as a discount on the first year of PRO Yearly. The offer screen states the discounted first-year price and the regular yearly price that applies from the second year. After the discounted period, the subscription renews at the regular yearly price until you cancel. You can dismiss the offer with “No thanks”. Offers are subject to Google Play eligibility, apply only to new subscriptions, and cannot be combined or applied retroactively to existing purchases.

06 —Billing and auto-renewal

  • Payment is charged to the payment method on your Google Play account when you confirm the purchase, or when a free trial ends.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period, at the then-current price, unless you cancel before the renewal date.
  • You can see your next renewal date and manage your plan at any time in Google Play.
  • If you switch between Monthly and Yearly, Google Play applies the change and any proration according to its rules.
  • Some payment methods can take time to complete. While a purchase is pending, the App says so, and PRO turns on as soon as Google Play confirms the payment.

07 —How to cancel

You can cancel at any time. Deleting the App does not cancel your subscription.

On your Android phone

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select Universal Remote.
  4. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the instructions.

You can also tap Manage subscription in the App’s Settings, or visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions while signed in to the same Google account.

After you cancel, PRO stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for (or until your trial ends), and you will not be charged again. Google Play may also let you pause or resubscribe.

08 —Refunds

Because payments are processed by Google Play, refunds are handled under Google Play’s refund policy.

  • Within 48 hours of a purchase, you can usually request a refund directly from Google Play (play.google.com → Order history → Request a refund).
  • After 48 hours, contact us at support@novasoftlabs.com with your Google Play order number (it starts with “GPA.”). We review each request fairly, for example if PRO did not work with your TV, and where appropriate we will issue a refund through Google Play.
  • Cancelling a subscription stops future charges but does not by itself refund past charges. Partial refunds for unused time in a billing period are not provided except where required by law.
  • If a purchase is refunded, PRO is removed from that Google account.

Nothing in this policy affects your statutory rights, including any right of withdrawal under the consumer law of your country.

09 —Price changes

We may change subscription prices for future billing periods. Google Play will notify you in advance as its policies require, and in some cases you must accept a price increase before it applies to you. If you do not want to continue at the new price, you can cancel before it takes effect. Price changes never affect a Lifetime purchase you have already made.

10 —Payment problems

If a renewal payment fails, Google Play may give you a grace period during which PRO stays active while you update your payment method, followed by an account hold during which PRO is paused. If the payment is not resolved, the subscription is cancelled. You can fix payment issues in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

11 —Restoring PRO and using it on other devices

PRO is tied to the Google account used to buy it, not to a separate Universal Remote account. To use PRO on a new or reset phone, install the App while signed in to the same Google account. PRO is detected automatically, or you can tap Restore on the upgrade screen. If no purchase is found, make sure the Play Store is signed in to the account that made the purchase.

The App remembers your PRO status on the phone so that it keeps working offline, and checks it again with Google Play when a connection is available.

12 —Changes to plans and this policy

We may add, change or remove plans, trials, offers and PRO features for new purchases. We will not reduce the core benefit of an active paid plan (an ad-free App) during a period you have already paid for. We may update this policy; the “Effective” date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be announced in the App or on its Google Play listing.
