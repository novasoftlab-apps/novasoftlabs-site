02 —What the app does

The App turns your Android phone into a Wi-Fi remote control for Roku TVs and Roku streaming players. Depending on your device, it lets you:

  • Find and connect to Roku devices on your network automatically, or by entering the device’s IP address;
  • Control your Roku with a D-pad or touchpad, power, home, back, volume, mute, input and playback keys, and a keyboard for typing into search and sign-in fields on the TV;
  • Open streaming apps installed on your Roku from the Apps tab, and tune antenna channels on Roku TVs from the Channels tab;
  • Use Private Listening to hear the TV’s sound through your phone and headphones.

We may add, change or remove features at any time, for example to support new Roku models or to keep up with changes Roku makes to its software.

03 —Compatibility

  • The App connects over your local Wi-Fi network. It does not use infrared and cannot control devices that are not connected to the network.
  • Your phone and Roku device must be on the same network. Guest networks, VPNs, “AP/client isolation” and some corporate or public networks can block the connection.
  • Some Roku settings, such as Control by mobile apps (Settings → System → Advanced system settings), must allow network access for the App to work.
  • Channels, input switching and power control are available only on Roku TVs and models that support them. Private Listening depends on your Roku model, its software and your Wi-Fi quality.
  • Roku can change its device software at any time, which may temporarily or permanently stop a feature from working. We do not guarantee that every feature works with every device.
  • The App is a convenience tool and is not a replacement for your original Roku remote. Keep your original remote for setup and recovery.

We recommend trying the App with your device, including during the free trial, before you pay for PRO.

06 —Private Listening

Private Listening plays the audio of what is on your TV through your phone. It runs as a foreground service with a visible notification for as long as it is on, and keeps playing while your screen is off. Audio quality and delay depend on your Wi-Fi network, phone and Roku device. The App does not record, save or share this audio. Please keep the volume at a safe level when using headphones.
