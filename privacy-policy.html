Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Welcome to Nova Soft Labs ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by Nova Soft Labs on the Google Play Store (collectively referred to as the "Applications"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Applications and the choices you have associated with that data. By downloading and using any of our Applications, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.
1

Information We Collect

Samsung Remote is designed to control your Samsung TV over your local Wi-Fi network. We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide this functionality:

  • Network information: Local IP addresses of devices on your Wi-Fi network to discover and connect to your Samsung TV. This data never leaves your device.
  • Device information: Basic device identifiers (e.g., Android version, device model) for diagnostic and compatibility purposes.
  • App usage data: Anonymous, aggregated usage statistics to help us improve the App (e.g., features used, crash reports). No personally identifiable information is included.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal contact information unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., when contacting support).

2

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To discover Samsung TVs on your local network and enable remote control functionality.
  • To diagnose technical issues, fix bugs, and improve App performance.
  • To understand how users interact with the App and identify areas for improvement.
  • To respond to your support requests or feedback (if you contact us).
3

Permissions Required

To function properly, the App may request the following Android permissions:

  • Local Network / Wi-Fi access: Required to discover and communicate with Samsung TVs on your home network.
  • Internet access: Used only for checking app updates or sending anonymous crash reports.
  • Receive boot completed (optional): To restore connection preferences on device restart, if enabled by the user.

We do not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage unless it is strictly required for a specific feature — in which case we will clearly notify you beforehand.

4

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share limited, non-identifiable data with trusted third-party service providers solely to operate and improve the App, such as:

  • Analytics providers (e.g., Firebase/Google Analytics) — for anonymous usage statistics.
  • Crash reporting tools (e.g., Firebase Crashlytics) — to detect and fix errors.

These providers are contractually obligated to handle data securely and only for the purposes we specify. They are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the public.

5

Data Storage & Security

Most data used by the App (such as discovered TV IP addresses and your preferences) is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

Any data that is transmitted (e.g., crash reports) is protected using industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS). We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6

Children's Privacy

Samsung Remote is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such information.
